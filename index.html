About the data

Figures will be calculated directly from the course CSV.

We include sale prices between $10,000 and $5,000,000 inclusive to reduce the effect of implausible transactions. A budget match means an eligible completed sale whose recorded price is no greater than your budget. City medians use all eligible sales in each city, not only the matching sales.

Rows represent completed sales, not current listings or necessarily unique properties. We never deduplicate by street address; the listing ID identifies sales. We do not use property type, days on market, lot size, tax, bathroom count or living area because these are not necessary for this analysis. Historical prices are not guarantees of present availability, and purchase price excludes financing and other costs.

Source: College-town home sales dataset, version 1.0 (2026). University of Illinois-Deloitte Foundation Center for Business Emerging Technologies. Data collected March 2026. Data description.
